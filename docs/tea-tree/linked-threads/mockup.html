Linked threads

Drag one thread onto the middle of another to link them. Linked threads then sit together in the list as one block, at the position of the most recently active one. Dropping in the gap between rows still reorders, as it does now.

1. Drag onto a thread

The row under the pointer gets an outline and a "Link" chip. Gaps between rows keep the current reorder line.

3. The way out, and seeing it

Right-click a linked row → "Unlink". Dragging a linked row out to a gap also unlinks it. The chat header shows the linked threads so you can jump between them.

Smallest model. Each thread gets one optional field, linkGroupId, stored and sent like pinnedAt is now. Dropping A on B gives A the group of B, or makes a new group of the two. Unlinking clears A's field, and a group left with one thread shows as a normal row. A group has no name and no order to keep. Rows in a group sort as they do now, and the block takes the place of its most recent row.

Where it shows. Option 2a needs no new state beyond that field. In 2b, the collapse state could be a client-only setting.
